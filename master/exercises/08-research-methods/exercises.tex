\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.1 -- Research Proposal}
Draft a 1-page proposal for a Master-thesis-sized OT-security research project. Cover: research question, prior work (3 citations), method, expected artefact, ethics + disclosure plan, evaluation criteria.
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.2 -- Method Selection}
For three classes of question (case, measurement, design science), give one OT-security example each that you would tackle with that method. Justify the choice in 3 lines per example.
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.3 -- Reproducibility Audit}
Pick one published ICS-security paper of your choice. Score its artefacts on a 0--5 scale across: code availability, data availability, container, README clarity, dependency pinning. Submit the score plus 1 paragraph commentary.
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.4 -- Mock Seminar Talk}
Prepare a 10-minute talk on an ICS-security paper of your choice. Slides limit: 8. Include: problem, gap, method, headline result, limits, next step. Be ready for hostile questions.
\end{taskbox}
