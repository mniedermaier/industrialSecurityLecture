\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.1 -- Mark scheme}
Strong proposals are testable in one Master-thesis cycle, have a clear evaluation criterion, and an explicit disclosure / ethics path. Deduct for vague ``investigate cybersecurity in ...''.
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.2 -- Mark scheme}
Award for matching example to method (e.g.\ measurement = a Shodan census; case = TRITON forensic walk-through; design science = a new GOOSE-signature tool).
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.3 -- Mark scheme}
Honest assessment beats charitable inflation. Bonus marks for pointing at a fix (the student volunteers a PR or note).
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 8.4 -- Mark scheme}
Pass requires a slide on limits + threats to validity, and at least one chart or screenshot from the paper. Bonus: a contrast slide showing where the paper's results have aged poorly.
\end{taskbox}
