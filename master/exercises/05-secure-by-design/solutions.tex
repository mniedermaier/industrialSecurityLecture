\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.1 -- Mark scheme}
Pass requires named zones (e.g.\ Plant, Process Control, Safety, Site IT) each with a justifiable SL-T (typically 3 for control, 4 for safety), per-conduit allowlists, and a defensible diode placement (typically safety$\to$site for telemetry egress).
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.2 -- Mark scheme}
Strong answers point to IEC 62443-4-1 evidence requirements (process maturity), CycloneDX/SPDX for SBOM, 90-day SLA with reasonable carve-outs, 10+ year support window, and signed downloads with vendor key rotation.
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.3 -- Mark scheme}
Award marks for: explicit pass/fail per step, traceable artefacts (vendor signature, PCAP, vuln report), and a backup/restore that completes within RTO.
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.4 -- Mark scheme}
Sensible sequencing (visibility before segmentation, segmentation before retirement), named owners, realistic cost bands, and risk reduction stated in process terms (downtime hours, safety events).
\end{taskbox}
