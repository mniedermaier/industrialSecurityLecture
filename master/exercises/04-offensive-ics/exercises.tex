\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 4.1 -- Rules of Engagement}
Draft a Rules of Engagement (ROE) document for a 1-week ICS penetration test of a fictional water utility. Include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Scope (what is in / out), with named devices and IP ranges.
  \item Timing (windows, holidays excluded, kill-switch).
  \item Approval chain (operations manager + CISO + safety engineer).
  \item Communications (start-of-day notice, daily status, end-of-day report).
  \item Forbidden actions (no DoS on safety systems, no writes to production PLCs).
\end{itemize}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 4.2 -- Recon Plan}
For the same utility, write a 1-page \emph{passive-first} recon plan:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item What you can learn from the corporate website + LinkedIn.
  \item What passive network monitoring would reveal in 24 hours.
  \item Which active probes you would attempt first, and why.
  \item How to detect when you have triggered an SOC alert.
\end{enumerate}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 4.3 -- Attack Path}
Pick one of: Modbus write, S7comm CPU stop, GOOSE spoof, OPC UA anonymous abuse. Document:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Pre-conditions (network access, credentials, equipment knowledge).
  \item Step-by-step execution against your lab.
  \item Process impact -- what would a real plant operator see?
  \item Detection opportunities at each step.
\end{enumerate}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 4.4 -- Report Writing}
Take any one finding from Exercise 4.3 and produce a 1-page finding write-up: title, executive summary (3 sentences), CVSS-E, evidence (PCAP excerpt or screenshot), reproduction (numbered), recommendation (this-quarter / this-year).
\end{taskbox}

\begin{warnbox}
Use only your lab. Reuse of lab findings against a real environment without authorisation crosses the line from training to crime.
\end{warnbox}
