\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 9.4 -- Programme Sketch (sample)}
RACI: CISO accountable; OT Security Lead responsible; Plant Manager consulted; CFO informed.\\
Year 1 priorities: complete asset inventory; segment IT$\leftrightarrow$OT with iDMZ; immutable backups; vendor remote-access broker; OT IDS; tabletop exercise.\\
KPIs: \% assets in CMDB with firmware version; mean time to detect (MTTD) on OT alerts; \% critical CVEs mitigated within SLA.
