\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 6.1 -- Advisory Analysis (mark scheme)}
Award marks for: correct CVE/CWE mapping (2), defensible environmental adjustments (3), realistic mitigation list including non-patch options (3), clear plain-language impact (2).
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 6.2 -- Lab Notes}
Expected differences:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Passive captures only protocols actually used; misses silent devices.
  \item Active scan may discover hidden services (\texttt{nmap -sV} reveals banners) but with higher risk of disturbing the PLC.
  \item OUI lookups (\texttt{ieee.org} or \texttt{macaddress.io}) reveal vendor.
\end{itemize}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 6.4 -- CVSS Reasoning}
With single-host reachability and MFA, modified attack vector drops from Network to Adjacent or Local, modified privileges required becomes High. The score can fall to $\sim$5.5--6.0. Whether that justifies P2 vs P1 depends on \emph{process consequence} -- if compromise leads to loss of safety view, treat as P1 regardless.
\end{taskbox}
