\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.1 -- ATT\&CK Mapping}
Pick one case study: Stuxnet, Ukraine 2015, Industroyer, TRITON, Colonial, or CyberAv3ngers/Unitronics.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Identify at least five MITRE ATT\&CK for ICS techniques used.
  \item For each, propose one detection that a defender could implement.
  \item Present the mapping as a three-column table: \emph{Tactic, Technique ID, Detection idea.}
\end{enumerate}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.2 -- Lab: Shodan Reconnaissance (ethical)}
See \texttt{labs/lab-05-shodan}.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Create a free Shodan account.
  \item Search for \texttt{port:502 country:DE} and report what kinds of systems appear.
  \item Repeat for \texttt{"Siemens" port:102} and \texttt{"Unitronics" port:20256}.
  \item In 5--10 lines: why are these systems exposed, what should asset owners do, what should ISPs/CERTs do?
\end{enumerate}
\begin{warnbox}
Read-only. Do \emph{not} connect to, log into, or scan any discovered system.
\end{warnbox}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.3 -- Threat Modelling}
For a small chemical plant with a single DCS and SIS:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Identify five plausible threat actors. Rank them by likelihood for this asset.
  \item For the top two, sketch an attack path from initial access to physical impact.
  \item Identify the choke points where a defender could break the chain.
\end{enumerate}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.4 -- Supply Chain Reasoning}
A vendor announces a critical RCE CVE in their PLC runtime, fix available next quarter.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item What compensating controls can you deploy now?
  \item Who in the organisation must be informed?
  \item How would you communicate the residual risk to a non-technical executive sponsor?
\end{enumerate}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.5 -- PIPEDREAM \& Volt Typhoon}
Read the Dragos PIPEDREAM/INCONTROLLER write-up \emph{and} the CISA Volt Typhoon advisory. Compare them in a 1-page brief:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Tooling -- is it custom malware or living-off-the-land?
  \item Targets -- which sectors and which assets are in scope?
  \item Detection -- which technique would surface each first in your environment?
  \item Defensive priority -- which one should the OT team treat as more urgent, and why?
\end{itemize}
\end{taskbox}

\begin{taskbox}{Exercise 5.6 -- Initial Access Audit}
For your own (or a fictional) plant, score each of the eight initial-access vectors from the slides (spearphish, vendor compromise, Internet-exposed PLC, USB, stolen credentials, vendor remote access, weak VPN, insider) on a 1--5 scale for \emph{exposure} (how easy is the door for an attacker) and \emph{detection} (how likely are we to notice). Pick the worst gap and propose one specific control.
\end{taskbox}
