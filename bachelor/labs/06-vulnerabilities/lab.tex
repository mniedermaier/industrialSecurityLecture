\labheader

\begin{goalbox}
Take a real, recent CISA ICS advisory and translate it for an asset owner: \emph{what it means, how bad it really is in their environment, what to do this week, and what to do this quarter.}
\end{goalbox}

\begin{stepbox}{1 -- Pick an advisory}
Browse \url{https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/ics-advisories} and pick one ICS advisory published in the last 60 days that affects a vendor you recognise.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{2 -- Decompose the advisory}
Read it and answer in writing:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item Vendor / product / affected firmware range
  \item CVSS v3.1 base score and full vector
  \item Vulnerability class (CWE)
  \item Pre-conditions for exploitation (network reachability, auth, user action)
  \item Stated impact and known exploitation
\end{itemize}
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{3 -- Environmental scoring}
For a small water utility with the affected product behind two firewalls and no Internet exposure: re-score with CVSS environmental modifiers and justify each change.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{4 -- Action list}
Produce a 5-line action list separated into \emph{this week} (compensating controls) and \emph{this quarter} (firmware update, change windows).
\end{stepbox}

\begin{deliverbox}
Hand in: a 1-page brief that a plant manager could read in 5 minutes -- summary, CVSS-E, this week, this quarter, residual risk.
\end{deliverbox}
