\labheader

\begin{goalbox}
For a fictional mid-sized German water utility, identify the applicable laws and standards, score five obligations against the controls covered in this course, and recommend three concrete improvements.
\end{goalbox}

\begin{stepbox}{1 -- Map the regulatory surface}
List the German and EU obligations that apply to a 600-thousand-customer water utility. Include:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item EU NIS2 (essential entity status)
  \item KRITIS / IT-SiG 2.0 (BSI)
  \item B3S Wasser/Abwasser (sectoral standard)
  \item Any data-protection or product regulations (DSGVO, CRA)
\end{itemize}
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{2 -- Pick five obligations}
Choose five concrete obligations (e.g.\ ``attack detection capability'', ``incident notification within 24 hours'') and assess current compliance: \emph{compliant / partial / non-compliant} with one-line justification each.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{3 -- Map to IEC 62443 and CSF}
For each obligation, cite the matching IEC 62443 system requirement (SR) and the matching NIST CSF subcategory.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{4 -- Recommend improvements}
Propose three concrete improvements with: \emph{rough cost, time horizon, owner.}
\end{stepbox}

\begin{deliverbox}
Hand in: a 1-page gap-analysis table (Obligation, Status, 62443 SR, CSF, Action) plus the three recommendations.
\end{deliverbox}
