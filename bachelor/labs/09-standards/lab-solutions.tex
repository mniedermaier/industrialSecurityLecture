\begin{goalbox}
Lecturer / TA reference: regulatory mapping for a German water utility, sample gap analysis, grading rubric. Numbers (CAPEX, OPEX, headcount) are indicative orders of magnitude, not procurement quotes.
\end{goalbox}

\begin{solutionbox}{Step 1 -- Regulatory surface (model answer)}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item \textbf{NIS2} -- Directive (EU) 2022/2555. Drinking-water supply is an Annex~I \emph{essential entity} sector. Transposition deadline was 17~Oct~2024; the German law (NIS2UmsuCG) governs the duties directly.
  \item \textbf{KRITIS} -- BSI-Kritisverordnung (BSI-KritisV). The drinking-water threshold is \mbox{500\,000} supplied persons; 600\,k crosses it, so the utility \emph{is} KRITIS-regulated and bound by \S\,8a~BSIG.
  \item \textbf{NIS2UmsuCG} -- the German NIS2 transposition act, superseding IT-SiG~2.0 for NIS2 duties (registration, incident reporting, governance accountability).
  \item \textbf{B3S Wasser/Abwasser} -- the sectoral security standard (\emph{branchenspezifischer Sicherheitsstandard}) recognised under \S\,8a~BSIG; treated as the de-facto control baseline for the audit.
  \item \textbf{CRA} -- Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. Applies whenever the utility procures a connected product (PLC, HMI, sensor, gateway) placed on the EU market; pushes vendor-side security duties.
  \item \textbf{DSGVO} -- applies to customer billing and contact PII, but \emph{not} to OT process data unless it can be linked to a natural person.
\end{itemize}
\end{solutionbox}

\begin{solutionbox}{Step 4 -- Three recommendations (cost / time / owner)}
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
  \item \textbf{Passive ICS IDS + OT-aware SIEM tier.} Deploy Claroty CTD or Nozomi Guardian on SPAN ports of the process LAN; forward alerts into the existing SIEM. \emph{CAPEX} EUR~80--150\,k, \emph{OPEX} EUR~15--25\,k/yr, \emph{time} 3--6 months, \emph{owner} CISO + OT lead. Closes the \S\,8a Abs.\,1a gap and feeds DE.CM-1 / DE.AE-3.
  \item \textbf{Brokered jump host for vendor access.} Replace the flat vendor VPN with a session broker (Cyolo, BeyondTrust PRA, or an audited open-source variant) enforcing MFA and full session recording. \emph{Cost} EUR~30--60\,k, \emph{time} 2--3 months, \emph{owner} IT-Security with plant-manager sign-off. Closes SR\,1.13 / SR\,2.1 / PR.AC-3.
  \item \textbf{Asset-inventory baseline.} Use the new IDS's passive discovery to enumerate the OT estate, reconcile against the CMDB, and formalise a quarterly reconciliation procedure. \emph{Effort only} (approx.\ 0.3 FTE for one month), \emph{owner} OT engineering team. Closes ID.AM-1 / SR\,2.1 and is a prerequisite for any meaningful patch-management plan.
\end{enumerate}
\end{solutionbox}

\begin{solutionbox}{Common student mistakes}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item Treating all \emph{essential-entity} obligations as IT-only obligations. NIS2 cyber-hygiene duties (Art.\,21) apply equally to OT.
  \item Citing DSGVO as applicable to OT process data. It only bites where personal data is involved (billing, customer portal).
  \item Recommending ``patch everything within 30 days'' without acknowledging vendor lifecycles, validated firmware images, or maintenance windows.
  \item Confusing the 500\,k KRITIS threshold with the NIS2 size cap -- NIS2 applies regardless of headcount for essential-entity sectors.
  \item Forgetting CRA on the procurement side: even a fully compliant operator inherits risk from non-CRA-conformant products.
\end{itemize}
\end{solutionbox}
