\begin{solutionbox}{Justification points students must articulate}
\textbf{Diode at L3$\to$L4.} The historian is the only legitimate egress from OT. A hardware diode enforces \emph{by physics} that no packet can flow back into L3, so a compromised corporate analytics box cannot pivot to PI and from there to SCADA. Stateful firewall rules degrade with misconfiguration; a diode does not.

\textbf{Jump host in L3.5.} Concentrates vendor / engineer remote access at one auditable choke point: MFA, session recording, brokered credentials. Without it every vendor laptop is a VPN endpoint into OT, and revoking access becomes a per-vendor scramble.

\textbf{No direct L1\,$\leftrightarrow$\,L4.} In IT the CIA priority is C\,>\,I\,>\,A; in OT it is A\,>\,I\,>\,C (availability and safety first). A direct conduit lets an IT-side incident (ransomware, AD outage, mis-applied patch) propagate to assets whose failure mode is physical. Each Purdue boundary is a deliberate latency/blast-radius break.

\textbf{NTP/PTP grandmaster in L3.} Forensics, IEC\,62443 logging, and certificate validity all need a common, trusted clock. Pulling time from L4/L5 couples OT availability to corporate DNS and routing.
\end{solutionbox}

\begin{solutionbox}{Common student mistakes -- mark down}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt]
  \item Arrow drawn directly from an L1 PLC (or L2 HMI) to an L4 or L5 service ``for reporting'' -- the conduit must terminate in L3.5.
  \item HMI placed in L3.5 (it belongs in L2; the iDMZ holds \emph{brokers}, not operator consoles).
  \item L3.5 drawn as a single firewall rule rather than a real zone with its own hosts, switching, and patch lifecycle.
  \item Bidirectional arrow on the historian replication conduit -- defeats the point of the diode.
  \item NTP sourced from \texttt{pool.ntp.org} on every PLC.
  \item AD trust extended from corporate into OT (single forest); kerberoasting on the IT side then yields domain admin in the plant.
  \item Vendor remote access shown as ``VPN to PLC'' with no jump host, no MFA, no recording.
\end{itemize}
\end{solutionbox}
