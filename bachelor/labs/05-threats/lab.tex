\begin{warnbox}
\textbf{Read-only contract.} Use Shodan only to observe what is publicly indexed.
You will \textbf{not}:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item connect to, log into, fingerprint, or scan any discovered system;
  \item publish IPs, hostnames, or other identifying data;
  \item interact with any infrastructure that is not your own.
\end{itemize}
\end{warnbox}

\begin{goalbox}
Quantify the scale of Internet-exposed industrial systems for one country and three product families. Reflect on responsibility and remedy.
\end{goalbox}

\begin{stepbox}{1 -- Account}
Register a free account at \url{https://account.shodan.io/register}.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{2 -- Queries}
Run each query in the Shodan web UI and record the approximate count plus three recurring banners:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item \texttt{port:502 country:DE}
  \item \texttt{"Siemens" port:102}
  \item \texttt{"Unitronics" port:20256}
  \item \texttt{"Schneider Electric" port:502}
\end{itemize}
\end{stepbox}

\begin{deliverbox}
Hand in a 1-page reflection answering:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item Who is responsible for each exposure (vendor, integrator, asset owner, ISP)?
  \item What should an asset owner do today on discovery?
  \item What can CERTs and regulators do at scale?
\end{enumerate}
Do not include any IPs, hostnames, or organisation names.
\end{deliverbox}
