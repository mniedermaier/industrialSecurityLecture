\begin{goalbox}
Build your first map of the IT/OT spectrum and the cyber-physical interfaces around you. No tools required -- pen and paper or any diagramming software.
\end{goalbox}

\begin{stepbox}{1 -- Spot the systems}
List eight systems within walking distance of your classroom (or building):
elevators, HVAC, badge readers, vending machines, lab equipment, fire panel,
projectors, parking barriers, electricity meter, etc. For each, decide:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item IT, OT, or hybrid (IIoT)?
  \item Worst-case consequence of compromise?
  \item Who owns / maintains it?
\end{itemize}
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{2 -- Draw a stakeholder map}
Pick one of the eight systems and draw the stakeholder map (operator, owner,
integrator, vendor, regulator). Mark which stakeholder cares about
\emph{availability}, \emph{integrity}, \emph{confidentiality} respectively.
\end{stepbox}

\begin{stepbox}{3 -- Reflection}
Write 5--7 lines: which stakeholder would push back against a security
control that interrupted normal operation, and why is that legitimate?
\end{stepbox}

\begin{deliverbox}
Hand in: the table from Step 1, the stakeholder diagram from Step 2, and the reflection.
\end{deliverbox}
